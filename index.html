Fair play

Every slot round in this prototype is settled on the server against house-configured odds and returns a fairness receipt; play-money credits only. Real-money gaming requires licensing, age checks, identity verification, secure payment processing, and independent RNG auditing.

Responsible play

Set your own limits and the server will hold you to them: a round that would breach them is refused before the stake leaves your balance. A limit of 0 means no limit. These are play-money credits, but the enforcement point is the one that would matter with cash.
